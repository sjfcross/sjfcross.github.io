Pilot Training

No-loss sparring drills for your S-class ships in X4: Foundations. Two teams, repeated rounds, and every pilot and crew member gains piloting and engineering, win or lose. Nobody can lose a ship.

Nexus Mods ↗ Steam Workshop ↗ All X4-Mods
🎖️

The Problem

In X4, pilots only get better in real fights, and real fights cost ships. A rookie pilot in a new fighter is the most likely thing in your fleet to die, which means they never get the experience that would keep them alive.

🥊

What It Is

A sparring ring. Your own S-class ships are split into Gold and Pink, fight each other in rounds, get repaired between rounds, and go home with the load they came with. Pilot and service crew are paid experience after every round.

🎯

The Goal

A training loop you can run from the map: recruit fresh crews, drill them, see the star gains in a results window, then move the trained people onto the ships that need them. Then recruit again.

Mechanics

How a round works

The ships are handed to two hidden factions that are hostile only to each other. Everyone else — your other ships, other mods' teams — stays neutral to them. When the drill ends, ownership comes back to you.

drill loop 
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  roster: your S ships (same sector, piloted) │
└──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                       v
   ┌───────────────┐       ┌───────────────┐
   │  GOLD faction │ <───> │  PINK faction │
   │  hull floor   │ fight │  hull floor   │
   │     10 %      │       │     10 %      │
   └───────┬───────┘       └───────┬───────┘
           └───────────┬───────────┘
                       v
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ hit to the out line (35 %) → OUT     │
   │ one team empty, or time up → END     │
   │ pay XP: pilot + service crew         │
   │ repair, refill missiles, 4 s, again  │
   └──────────────────┬───────────────────┘
                      v
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ release: hull, shields, owner, commander and │
│ missile load restored  →  results window     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The rules of the ring

  • OUT A ship hit down to the out line (default 35 % hull) is out at once. Checked on every hit, with a 1-second poll as backup. It turns grey and waits.
  • END The round ends when one team has nobody left, or when the time limit (default 3 min) runs out. A draw still pays.
  • XP Everyone is paid from the mod's own experience category: the pilot as an individual, the service crew as a group. Win or lose.
  • FIX Ships are repaired — including shot-off engines and weapons — and missiles and flares topped back up. The next round starts 4 seconds later, from where they are.
  • RUN No fleeing. The attack order is a priority order, so a ship with low shields or low morale isn't sent off to boost away or dock mid-round.

Safe mode

Why nobody can die — and why that was the hard part

Every ship in a drill fights with a 10 % hull floor. But a floor makes the game treat a ship as indestructible, and the vanilla AI refuses to attack indestructible targets. The first version's ships just circled each other. The fix is three small patches to vanilla AI scripts that lift that rule only between a Gold and a Pink ship.

Vanilla script What the patch changes
interrupt.disengage Don't break off and "learn" the opponent as indestructible.
lib.target.selection Don't skip it when picking a target.
order.fight.attack.object May still turn on whoever is shooting at it.
interrupt.attacked Not patched — it already ignores ships on a priority order, which is what stops fleeing and crew bail checks mid-round.
✅ Fails safe
If a game update ever breaks the patches, the ships only circle. A round with zero hits prints a red chat warning.
✅ Losses are loud
A lost ship sets off a red ticker, a chat line and a logbook entry, and by default stops the drill.
✅ Close calls counted
A ship that goes out at 10 % hull or less is announced in yellow and logged, with the lowest hull ever seen at out.
⚠️ One-burst risk
A single burst that takes a ship from above the floor to zero before the hit event fires would kill it. Unlikely between S fighters at 35 %, not impossible.

In-game UI

Run it from the map

Everything is driven from the map's Property Owned menu and the right-click menu. Chat commands exist too, but you never need them.

Putting a drill together

  • 1 Right-click one of your S ships (or drag-select several) → Pilot Training → Put on Gold team / Put on Pink team.
  • 2 The map opens Property Owned → Pilot Training: both teams, with a column button that cycles Status, Pilot skills and Crew points gained.
  • 3 Run 3 rounds, Run 5 rounds or Run until stopped. A status line shows the round, time left and how many to go; the tab refreshes every 5 s.
  • 4 Stop drill any time. Every ship goes home with its hull, shields, owner, fleet commander and missile load restored.

After the drill

  • ★ Results window opens by itself. Each person's piloting and engineering was recorded at the start, so it shows the real gain in stars. Ships are sorted by total gain.
  • → Work somewhere else for me on every row opens the vanilla map picker — choose a ship or station and a job, and the game transfers them.
  • + New recruits fills empty pilot seats and crew places on docked roster ships with fresh recruit-level people, 7,500 Cr a head.
  • ⇪ Take the training group aboard: right-click a big ship and every pilot and crew member on the roster is assigned there as service crew, best pilots first.

Loop  Recruit → Train → Take group aboard → Recruit again.

Chat commands

/ptrain

The chat window does everything the tab does, plus the settings. Settings can only be changed between drills.

Command What it does
/ptrainHelp plus current state.
/ptrain gold ABC-123 DEF-456Put ships on Gold (or pink). target uses the ship you have HUD-targeted.
/ptrain run · run 5 · run loopOne round, N rounds, or until stopped.
/ptrain stopEnd now and give every ship back. An unfinished round pays nothing.
/ptrain set floor 35Hull percentage that counts as "shot down" (5–90).
/ptrain set time 3Round time limit in minutes.
/ptrain set warp onWarp both teams to start lines 6 km apart between rounds (default: fight on where they are).
/ptrain set missiles refill · banTop missiles up before every round (default), or strip them for the drill. Either way every ship goes home with the load it came with.
/ptrain set onloss stop · continueWhat a destroyed roster ship does to the drill.
/ptrain losses · resultsLoss log and close calls; reopen the last results window.
/ptrain rescueEmergency: every ship still owned by a training faction comes back to you.

Experience

Tuning the training speed

The mod adds its own experience category instead of borrowing a vanilla one, so the rate can be tuned without touching anything else in the game.

libraries/experiences.xml (shape) 
<!-- own category: ptrain_bout -->
<experience id="ptrain_bout">
  <!-- pilot (individual) -->
  piloting    falloff 0.83  cap 15
  engineering falloff 0.68  cap 15
  <!-- service crew (group) -->
  same rates, group cap 15  (vanilla: 8)
</experience>

// per round: XP × $XPFactor
normal  = 0.5
fast    = 1.0   // "Fast training" checkbox

Normal vs. fast

  • OPT Extension Options → Pilot Training → Fast training (double experience). Off is normal (factor 0.5), on is fast (1.0). Saved with the game.
  • CAP The crew group cap is 15 instead of vanilla's 8, so a full service crew learns too, not just the first eight.
  • REF For scale: a vanilla hard kill pays piloting 0.43 to the pilot and 0.21 (cap 8) to the crew. Higher falloff means each level slows down less.

Install

Requirements and known gaps

Subscribe on the Steam Workshop or download from Nexus Mods — same mod, pick one. Then restart X4 completely: the new factions and experience category are only read at game start.

Needs

  • API SirNukes Mod Support APIs — chat commands, options page and results window.
  • UIX kuertee's UI Extensions — the map tab. Either build (Steam or Nexus) works since 1.01. Without it the chat commands still work.
  • kMEx Runs side by side with kuertee's Military Exercises, which inspired it. Both patch the same AI checks; both load orders were tested.
⚠️ Standing orders
A ship's own default order is cancelled for the drill. It comes back to its fleet commander, or gets Hold Position.
⚠️ Saving mid-drill
Stores those ships as owned by a training faction. Load and /ptrain stop fixes it. Never uninstall with a drill running.
⚠️ Same sector
Every ship on the roster must be in one sector when you start. One drill at a time.
⚠️ S-class only
Owned by you, with a pilot, no subordinates, and not the ship you're flying.
