Who lives where, right now

24 hours

A day in the cupboard

The controller runs the climate zone by a short list of rules. The simulation plays a whole day of them in about a minute and a half: the clock races through quiet stretches and slows down whenever something happens.

  • At night (23:00–07:00) the fogger runs for the first 3 minutes of every hour and the fan for 4 minutes.
  • At 90 % humidity the fogger is blocked, and only allowed again below 85 %.
  • Three bad sensor reads and the fogger is forced off. Try "Pull the sensor".
  • The LED panels run on their own timer, 12:00–22:30. The Sea Monkey bubbler is manual: 10 or 30 minutes.

The rules are the real ones (firmware v2.4). The humidity curve is an illustrative model, not a measurement.
