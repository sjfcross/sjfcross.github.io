Enhanced Bailing 9.x

Any fight in the galaxy can now leave a claimable hull — not just yours. Abandoned hulls self-destruct after an hour so space doesn't fill up with wrecks, and word reaches you when one bails nearby.

Nexus Mods ↗ Steam Workshop ↗ All X4-Mods
💥

The Problem

In the unmodded game, an NPC crew only abandons ship if you are the one shooting. Every other fight — Xenon raids, pirate ambushes, faction wars — ends in explosions and nothing else.

🛟

What It Is

A rewrite of MoonShadowFan's 2021 EnhancedBailing for X4 9.00+, published with their permission. NPC-vs-NPC fights can leave empty hulls drifting, and a cleanup timer keeps their number in check.

🎯

The Goal

A galaxy where a fight next door is worth flying to. Measured defaults, thirteen in-game settings, a reachability filter so you only hear about hulls you could get to, and an uninstall that is just deleting the folder.

What it changes

From bail to wreck to rumour

The mod never touches derelicts placed by the game, by DLCs or by other mods. Only ships that bailed under this mod ever go on the cleanup list.

Six changes

  • 1 NPC-vs-NPC ships can be fully abandoned. The headline feature.
  • 2 More frequent, bigger bail rolls — 5 s apart instead of 30 s, and whole-crew walkouts instead of at most three.
  • 3 Abandoned hulls self-destruct after 1 hour, swept by a 60-second collector.
  • 4 Demand surrender from any size ship over comms, not just S and M.
  • 5 Hull rumours — a logbook entry with a click-to-map link, and one quiet line in the ticker.
  • 6 Big ships can hold out, or must be boarded — two optional settings, both off by default.

Why a rewrite

A patch that silently matched nothing

The 2021 mod patched vanilla scripts by position. In 9.00 that position points at a completely different cue, so its remove and add both matched zero nodes — and the bail overhaul had been a silent no-op for years. The 9.x version anchors every patch by cue name and is built as a delta on the current vanilla block, so the 9.00 features the old code predates are all kept.

selector: 2021 vs 9.x 
<!-- 2021: by position — breaks when cues move -->
sel="…/cue[24]/actions/do_if[3]"

<!-- 9.x: by name — md/notifications.xml -->
<replace sel="//cue[@name='PlayerOwnedShipAttacks']
  /actions/do_if[@value='$target.iscapturable']">

<!-- 9.x: by name — md/conversations.xml -->
<replace sel="//cue[@name='DefaultComm']/actions
  /do_if/set_value[@name='$maysurrender']">

Fixed along the way

  • BUG A missing tag.mandatory guard that destroyed equipment vanilla protects.
  • BUG A cleanup routine that could detonate a hull an NPC had already claimed and re-crewed.
  • +4 Four more bugs from the original, all documented in the engineering log.
  • NEW The settings page and the cleanup collector were written from scratch.

Settings

Thirteen dials, live

Options → Extension Options → Enhanced Bailing — the main Options menu, not the Extensions tab. Sliders take effect immediately and are saved per game. The settings page needs SirNukes Mod Support APIs; without it the mod runs on its defaults.

Too much, or too little?

  • MAX Damping 1 is the mod as originally designed: roughly 7–8 bails an hour galaxy-wide. The default of 2 is deliberately half that.
  • 1 Too much? NPC-vs-NPC damping → 3 or 4.
  • 2 Then time between bail rolls → 15 s.
  • 3 Then nerve of the crew → 4. Each step roughly halves the volume, and they stack.
  • XEN Too many free Xenon ships? Raise Xenon resistance. At 1 they were 5 of every 8 hulls in testing.

Hull rumours

A lead, not a taunt

An abandoned ship makes no noise and the galaxy is large. So the mod tells you — quietly, and only about hulls worth the trip. It knows when each hull will die, so the report carries a countdown, and hulls that would expire before you could plausibly get there are never reported at all.

The range slider

  • 0Off.
  • 1Your own sector.
  • 2Within 3 jumps (default) — roughly the part of the map you actually operate in.
  • 3Anywhere you've discovered. Quieter than it sounds: a hull twelve jumps out needs ~38 minutes left to be worth mentioning.
  • 4Everywhere — including sectors you've never seen. A small spoiler for a lead you probably can't use.
Logbook off · ticker on
You hear a hull is out there and roughly where, and go find it. The lead without the waypoint.
Logbook on · ticker off
Nothing ever interrupts you. Rumours pile up under News for when you feel like reading.
Both off
Same as range 0. The cleanup timer carries on — "reaper only" without touching the lifetime.
News or nothing
Switching a channel back on doesn't replay hulls that bailed while it was off.

Big ships

How long, and whether at all

The crew draw is scale-free: both bounds are proportional to the ship's own crew, so every class from M upward empties in about 2.3 successful rolls — a 612-berth Ark takes no longer than a 41-berth miner. Two optional settings change that. With both off the mod behaves exactly as before.

⏳

Big ships hold longer

A slider from 1 to 6 that divides both bounds by a class factor. S and M are untouched. At the top setting an L crew takes about eight successful rolls to break, and an XL about ten. It decides how long a big crew holds out.

⚓

Big traders must be boarded

Capital warships have always kept their last crew member. L/XL miners, freighters and builders didn't, so they could be found empty. Tick this and every L/XL hull keeps one — it decides whether the ship can be found empty at all.

🧭

Which one you want

If you want something left to board, use the checkbox. A long enough fight still empties a freighter at any slider setting, and below 30 % hull the whole crew can leave at once. The slider is flavour on top.

Install, uninstall, compatibility

Delete the folder — that's the whole uninstall

Drop enhanced_bailing_9x into the X4 extensions folder, or subscribe on the Workshop. Safe to add to a save already in progress. Needs X4 9.00 or later.

✅ No save dependency
The save records no dependency on the mod. Patches are applied in memory at load, so the next load without it runs stock.
✅ Two files, by name
Patches only notifications.xml and conversations.xml, anchored by cue name. Timelines touches different parts — no conflict.
✅ Protected ships stay put
Plot ships, mission ships and every ship in a military exercise never bail — so your own crews can't walk out of a training drill for real.
⚠️ History stays history
"Won't corrupt your save" isn't "won't change your galaxy". Hulls that bailed or were claimed stay that way. Removing the mod stops the cleanup timer, so queued hulls become permanent.
⚠️ Rival bail rewrites
Any other mod that rewrites the vanilla bail block will conflict. Nothing else should.
🔧 Troubleshooting
Turn on Write debug log: [EB] loaded means it runs, eval lines name the gate that stopped each roll.
